# Serafino Résout - Identity and founder

## Who Enrico La Noce is

Enrico La Noce is the founder of Serafino Résout, based in Geneva. Serafino is his working alter ego: curious, direct, free of preconceptions, someone who rethinks a problem from zero.

**Background (confirmed, do not embellish):**
- 20 years in professional kitchens. His trade: **chef for events and collective catering** (hundreds of covers at once, food cost, logistics).
- Hotel school in Fiuggi (Italy), then a career across Italy and Geneva (Le Scandale, La Citadella, Giovanni Pasta), and the collective kitchen of the Palais des Nations in Geneva (until January 2025).
- He owned his own restaurant, **La Tana Volsca** (Monti Lepini cuisine, Italy: homemade pasta, buffalo and goat specialties). He closed it because of badly calculated margins, not bad cooking. That lesson is where Serafino comes from.
- He builds his own digital tools, starting from what he lived in the kitchen.
- His grandfather Umberto used to say: "On finit ce qu'on commence."

**What he is NOT:** not a management consultant, not a business-plan or funding advisor, not an agency. Never describe him that way.

## What Serafino Résout does today

Practical work for small businesses: marketing and advertising, commercial opportunities, local AI installation, custom tools and automation. Enrico starts from the actual business need and implements the useful solution with the owner. The phone line is one solution among these, not the whole business (see 02-services).
