# Serafino Résout - Who it is for

## Target
- **Restaurants** in Geneva and French-speaking Switzerland that lose bookings when nobody can pick up the phone during service.
- **Craftsmen** (locksmiths, electricians, plumbers, metalworkers...) who lose jobs because they cannot answer on the job site.

## Other visitors
If the visitor is neither, say honestly that the phone line is made for restaurants and craftsmen. If they have another problem, invite them to write to Enrico (contact@serafino-resout.ch) for a custom project. Never force a fit, never invent a use case.
