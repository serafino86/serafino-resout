## La Ligne (telephone line for restaurants and craftsmen) - monthly subscription
When the phone rings during service, La Ligne answers in French, asks first name, number of people, day and time, and sends the request to the owner's phone; the owner confirms or refuses. The owner keeps his number: calls he does not take go to the line. For a craftsman it notes the need, the address and the urgency.
- **Artisan: 79 CHF par mois** (400 minutes of calls per month). **Restaurant: 149 CHF par mois** (800 minutes). **Pro: dès 249 CHF par mois** (for busier houses, minutes agreed together).
- Sans engagement: résiliable chaque mois, préavis 30 jours, by e-mail, no exit fee. If the minutes of the plan are exceeded, Enrico warns first, nothing extra is billed without agreement. Enrico installs it himself on your number.
- Demo: call 022 510 55 86 with the demo app open. It is a demo, no message is sent to a customer. No restaurant uses it in production yet.

## 2. L'IA chez vous (private AI installed on your computer)
Enrico installs an AI on your computer, at home or in the shop, sets it up for your everyday tasks and spends half a day with you so you can use it alone (Atelier IA sur votre métier). Your documents stay on your machine. No subscription.
- **Dès 490 CHF la demi-journée, hardware not included** (installation + hands-on help, the tool stays installed).
- Honest limits: it is slower than an online service and more limited on hard tasks; on a computer that is too weak it is useless. That is why Enrico checks your machine first and tells you before starting if it does not suffice. Sometimes the right answer is to keep your online subscription.
- The answers must be re-read, like an apprentice's work. If a function needs internet, he says so beforehand.
- It can also answer from your own documents (prices, opening hours...). This website chat is NOT an example of private AI: it runs online and the visitor's messages are sent to an external AI provider, Groq (United States). Do not type sensitive personal data in the chat.
